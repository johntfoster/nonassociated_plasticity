# Author profile for repository edits

## Use

Apply this profile to manuscript prose, captions, tables, source comments, and
manuscript-development documentation. The user's explicit instructions and
accepted notation take precedence.

## Voice and structure

- Begin with the physical system or mathematical task.
- State the manuscript's contribution positively and precisely.
- Use first-person plural for paper-level actions. In derivations, let the
  balance, free-energy inequality, kinematic split, or constitutive quantity be
  the subject.
- Give each technical paragraph one identifiable task: claim, mathematical or
  physical basis, and implication.
- Define notation at its first substantive use. Keep derivations
  equation-forward: physical purpose, equation, local definitions, consequence,
  and, where useful, a limiting interpretation.

## Plasticity terminology

- Preserve the distinction between true-plastic deformation and plastic
  distension, their conjugate forces, and their separate contributions to
  plastic power.
- Distinguish an associated multi-mechanism theory in its generalized-force
  space from a projected, apparent non-associated description in true-stress
  space.
- State assumptions, constitutive choices, derived restrictions, and limiting
  reductions explicitly.
- Reuse the manuscript's established notation. Do not introduce a helper
  variable or force map unless it materially improves clarity and the user has
  approved it.

## Prose constraints

- Use ordinary technical English and moderate-length sentences.
- Avoid conversational work-note phrasing, drafting history, unsupported
  novelty claims, and rhetorical contrasts with prior work.
- Describe cited work affirmatively, then state what this manuscript derives.
- Use negation only for a mathematical condition, operative assumption,
  necessary limitation, or distinction between quantities that could otherwise
  be confused.
- Treat displayed equations as grammatical parts of their sentences. Use
  `align` for multi-step derivations and avoid manual delimiter sizing.

## Review checklist

Before completing an edit, confirm that new notation is locally defined,
equations and citations are preserved unless intentionally changed, and the
edited prose explains the physical role and immediate consequence of each
displayed result.
