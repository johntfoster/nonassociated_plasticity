# Vision

This repository develops *Apparent non-associative plasticity from true
deformation and distension*. The manuscript derives a finite-deformation,
single-phase plasticity theory in which true-plastic deformation and plastic
distension are distinct internal mechanisms.

The manuscript is the sole authoritative project artifact. Work in this
repository is limited to its LaTeX source, bibliography, reference material,
and manuscript-development instructions and skills. The immediate research
goals are to sharpen the thermodynamic derivation, document the precise
relationship to Drucker--Prager plasticity, and prepare a self-contained
theory paper.
