# Collins and Houlsby (1997)

## Source

- I. F. Collins and G. T. Houlsby, “Application of thermomechanical principles to the modelling of geotechnical materials,” *Proceedings of the Royal Society of London. Series A*, 453(1964), 1975–2001 (1997).
- DOI: `10.1098/rspa.1997.0107`
- BibTeX key: `collins1997`
- Local PDF: `references/pdfs/collins-houlsby-1997-thermomechanical-geotechnical-materials.pdf`

## Relevance

The paper develops a thermodynamic framework for frictional geomaterials from energy and dissipation potentials. It connects stress-dependent dissipation, friction, dilation, and non-associated plastic flow, and formulates the theory using Gibbs free energy. Sections 4–5 and equations (5.8)–(5.14) are the portions cited in the manuscript.
